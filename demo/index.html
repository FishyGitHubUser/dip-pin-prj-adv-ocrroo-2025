Accessible Video OCR

This accessible demo opens your device's file explorer. Select a video file to continue to the app page where you can play the video, change playback speed, upload another video, and paste or view OCR results. All pages are navigable via the keyboard keys: ARROW-UP, ARROW-DOWN, ARROW-LEFT, ARROW-RIGHT, Tab, Enter, and Space.

Tip: Press Enter or Space, or click anywhere on the page to open your file explorer.
